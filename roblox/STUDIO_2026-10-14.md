## 10. Luckiest Guy text centred in every menu (CAPS_CENTER, 19:05, the owner)
- **The cause:** the font draws its capitals 0.14 em above the middle of its line. The old fix (CHUNKY_DOWN) worked on
  touch screens only, plus a few hand-placed 1-5 px offsets.
- **The fix:** `UI.capsAuto(PlayerGui)` puts a UIPadding (top +d, bottom -d, d = 0.14 x line height) on every centred
  label of that font in a ScreenGui, on every device.
  - Labels that place their own words are opted out with `NoCapsDrop` (the PLAY button, the next-goal button).
- **Hand offsets removed:**
  - the button label's touch-only drop;
  - the chip text, NEW pill, window ribbon title (17→13 in Client, 16→11 in MenuKit);
  - the HUD pill, the Shop timer pill and the odds box;
  - the MoreUI / Trade / Playtime / HammersUI / MenuKit bar labels' 1-2 px;
  - the Admin button.
  - SpinUI `slim` now centres its title and line as one block.
- **Audited by script on PC and phone:** Spin, Store (all tabs), Shop, Jobs, Upgrades, Rebirth, Company, Places, Garage,
  Playtime, Inventory, Index, Codes, Trade and the HUD. No label is off-centre except the Index section headers, which
