## AUTO BUY BEST (replaces BEST BUY)
Client.UpgradesUI, marker `AUTO_BUY_BEST` / `AUTO_NEXT`.
- A slim strip (64 px) pinned at the top of the Upgrades window; the YOU HAVE strip sits under it.
- Buys the CHEAPEST upgrade you have the materials for. Tap = one; hold = keeps buying, faster and faster, until the cash
  runs out; then the button turns grey (NO CASH). The strip survives redraws so a held button keeps working.
- The upgrade it will buy is outlined in gold in the list, with an AUTO NEXT tag.
