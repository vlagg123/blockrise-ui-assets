How: a fresh profile played from the tutorial (crate, fence, gloves, 10 reps, excavator, laborer, home), then a bot
(LocalScript) that played by itself for ~40 minutes: best or random contract → walk to the site → click until done,
City Tower hits, training, buying + opening crates, departments, properties, workers, gear, machines, house, missions,
gifts, spin, Rebirth + Star Shop, helmet, and 3 random windows per cycle. Passes and boosts switched on/off in the middle,
rain, random events, City Tower started again and again.

Result: 109 contracts built by the bot (13 + 96, 0 failed), ~40 City Towers joined, 2 Rebirths, 600+ windows opened, 0 errors on the
server, 0 errors on the client, memory flat (server 23-25k instances, the UI about 2-3k), no NaN anywhere in the save.
All 16 windows open with content (no "Loading"); 1,600 random remote calls with wrong types (NaN, inf, huge strings,
tables, Instances) left the cash and Gems right; 8 spins / 6 gifts / 6 mission claims at the same moment: only one counts.

Fixes:
- NAN_GUARD (critical): CompanyAction "sell" with qty NaN made the cash and the material NaN (a broken save). Sell is fixed
  (NaN = 1, inf = all you have); addMoney / addGems refuse a NaN or infinite amount; on load every NaN / infinite number in
  a save becomes 0; trade-up rarity NaN refused cleanly.
- MISSIONS_DAY: progress first makes today's missions (after midnight it went into yesterday's list until the window
  was opened; then today's progress started from 0).
- CARD_ONE: a new PROJECT COMPLETE / MEGA card replaces the one still open (they piled up darker and darker).
- PASS_OFF: a pass that is gone (refund, admin panel) switches Auto Builder / Auto Train off.
Note: every crew worker is about 240 instances (14 workers = 3,400): fine now, worth slimming if servers get full.
