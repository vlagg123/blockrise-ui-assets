## Fast double taps (PRESS_SAFE, MenuKit)
- Measured in Play with real mouse clicks: after a tap the window was drawn again 2-3 times (the tap's own prediction,
  the server's answer, the crate count), 120 and 280 ms after the tap. A redraw between the press and the release of

  Company), the Rebirth window.
- Tested with real clicks: Golden Crate double click = 2 crates, 5 fast clicks = 5; Upgrades double click = 2 levels,

## 2026-10-08 (03:10) — DRIVE_RACE: a new car would not move until you got out and in again

- Cause: on a live server a freshly spawned car reaches the client over several frames, and the server seats you at once.
  The client's `Seated` handler (`Drive.start`) ran before the car's `PrimaryPart` / movers had arrived, returned
  silently, and nothing tried again until the next `Seated` (getting out and back in). Studio has no latency, so it worked
  there.
- Fix (StarterPlayerScripts.Drive): `start()` waits up to 6 s for the car's root and its `Drive` / `Upright` movers (and
  checks you are still in that seat); plus a Heartbeat fallback every 0.25 s: in your own car's driver seat but not
  driving (or moved straight from one car into another) -> start now.
- Tested in Play: normal DRIVE spawn drives; a simulated missed start (Seated while the car was not recognisable yet)
  is picked up by the fallback and the car drives with W (48 studs/s, 33 studs in 1.5 s). Console clean.

## 2026-10-08 (03:45) — LEVEL_REBIRTH: the Rebirth needs a Level, not saved cash

Owner's idea: saving a huge sum only to lose it at the Rebirth felt bad; a Level lets you spend your cash as you like.

- **Rule:** Rebirth N needs **Level 12, 35, 55, 75, 100, 125, then +20 levels each** (Company.RebirthLevels /
  RebirthLevel(n)), plus the zone's top building built once (unchanged: Corner Shop, Distribution Center, Grand Hotel...).
  No cash is paid; the cash resets at the Rebirth like before (the new run's head start is unchanged).
- **Why these numbers:** the Level is kept through Rebirths and XP has no multipliers, so each step is sized on the
  zone's XP: XP_total(L) = sum 120 x l^1.45. L12 = 19.4K XP (the tutorial + ~25 Corner Shops); 12->35 = ~270K
  (~30 Distribution Centers, 9K XP each); 35->55 = ~590K (~25 Grand Hotels, 24K); 55->75 = ~1.0M (~27 Skyscrapers,
  38K); 75->100 = ~1.9M (~32 HQs, 60K); 100->125 = ~2.8M (~28 Spires, 100K); +20 levels after = ~2.7M+ (~27 Spires).
  Close to the old cash pacing for Rebirth 1 (~36 Corner Shops for $150K).
- **Bar:** XP from the last Rebirth's Level to this one's (Company.RebirthProgress), never goes down; the 1%..75% prizes
  stay on it. Text "LEVEL 9 / 12", "Rebirth needs Level 12 — you are Level 9", "10,167 XP to go"; live on Level / XP.
- **Stars:** 3 + 2 x N + 1 per doubling of the zone's cash scale you EARNED this run (spent cash counts now).
- HUD badge = Level reached + top building; Road steps "Level 12 → Rebirth 1" etc.; the window's note lists the levels
  and says the cash resets ("spend it").
- Tested in Play: L9 -> bar 0.476, NOT YET, 10,167 XP to go; L12 + Corner Shop -> REBIRTH!; the Rebirth went through
  (Rebirths 1, Level kept 12, cash reset to the head start, next = Level 35, bar from 0); a second Rebirth at L12 refused
  ("Rebirth needs Level 35 — you are Level 12"). Console clean.

## 2026-10-08 (04:40) — LOOP_V7: "Strength is your power, and it is yours for good"

The owner played v5 and found the loop flat (same house again and again, the Training Yard pointless, the crew doing
everything, Strength lost at the Rebirth). What was really wrong, in the numbers: Strength went into a log curve
(10 million Strength = x6.8 power), so Strength changed nothing you could feel; the crew ignored it and worked at x4
your output; the best play was one building forever.

- **Power = 1 + (Strength / 40)^0.55** (Config.StrengthMult; the v5 curve kept as StrengthMultV5): 1,000 = x6.9,
  100K = x75, 10M = x938 (was x2.2 / x4.5 / x6.8). Training and building visibly make you stronger.
- **Strength is kept through the Rebirth** (Main.resetRun; the Rebirth window lists it under YOU KEEP). Gear, crew,
  machines, properties and upgrades still reset.
- **The crew and the machines use your full power** (Economy "crew" no longer divides the Strength out) at lower rates:
  laborer 0.3, builder 0.6, foreman 0.4 of your power a second (were 1.4 / 3.4 / 2.4 of your hammer); machines 1.2 / 1.6 /
  1.2 (were 4.5 / 6 / 4.5). A full crew ≈ 1.5-2x your own hitting, not 4x.
- **Every contract's work rescaled** so its FIRST build (at its Strength requirement, with its crew requirement) takes as
  long as in v5, and every build after that gets faster as you grow: factor = new total output / old total output
  (x1 fence … x4.1 Corner Shop … x81 Distribution Center … x2151 Spire). Measured in Play: S = 4000 -> PowerMult 13.6,
  CrewMult 13.6.
- **HOT contract** (Main): one per zone pays x2 for 10 minutes, then another (ReplicatedStorage Hot_<zone>, HotEnds);
  Job Board chip "🔥 HOT x2", HUD chip "HOT x2: CORNER SHOP 8:11", the ACCEPTED banner says it.
- **VARIETY streak** (Main, d.VarietyStreak / d.LastContract, attribute VarietyStreak): every contract different from
  your last one +20% pay, up to +100%; the same one twice resets it. Job Board: "VARIETY +40%" / "SAME AGAIN: streak
  resets", the row's cash shows what THIS build pays; HUD chip "VARIETY +40% CASH". Reset at the Rebirth.
- Tested: HOT Corner Shop + streak 1 paid 4200 x 2 x 1.2 (+25% speed); fence as the 2nd different building paid
  60 x 1.4 x 1.25 = 106; fence again -> streak 0; Rebirth kept Strength 4000 (gear back to 1). Console clean.
