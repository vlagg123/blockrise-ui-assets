# BlockRise Empire - Studio changes, 2026-10-08 (01:30)

## Fast double taps (PRESS_SAFE, MenuKit)
- Measured in Play with real mouse clicks: after a tap the window was drawn again 2-3 times (the tap's own prediction,
  the server's answer, the crate count), 120 and 280 ms after the tap. A redraw between the press and the release of
  the next tap took that button away, so the tap was lost (double click on a crate = 1 crate).
- `K.pressing()` / `K.afterRelease(key, fn)`: a window is never drawn again while a short press (< 0.4 s) is in
  progress; the redraw comes right after the release (one per window, the newest wins). A long hold (hold to buy) is
  drawn at once. Used by: the Shop (`M.Show` redraws), the Inventory (`c.redrawInventory`), `K.instant` (Upgrades,
  Company), the Rebirth window.
- Tested with real clicks: Golden Crate double click = 2 crates, 5 fast clicks = 5; Upgrades double click = 2 levels,
  5 clicks = 4 (the 5th needs Copper: refused, right).

## Collections on the Shop cards (COLLECTION_HOW)
- Pirate Cove / Jungle Temple: "600 Gems · Collect 3: +5% luck"; Legends: "3,000 Gems · Collect 3: +5% luck"
  (3 of a set's 4 hammers = +5% luck). Exclusive Crate: "Robux only · Legendary or better".

## Odds in % before you buy (ODDS_PCT, Roblox paid random items rules)
- Before: the crates showed "1 in X" only, the Store cards the 2 best rarities, the Exclusive "1 in ???", and the
  window with every % (`cratePopup`) was not opened from anywhere.
- Now: an ODDS button on every crate card (Shop: on the picture; Store: the card's corner) opens the odds window:
  every rarity in % (4 decimals past the first digit that isn't 0) and "1 in X", every hammer's own %, the exact % of
  a "1 in ???" hammer (Thunderclap, Chrono), the luck counted, and "The chances are rounded, so they may not add up to
  exactly 100%." The hover box over the bar has a % column too. No "1 in ???" for the Exclusive rarity any more (the
  Exclusive Crate says 1 in 33).
- Already right: PolicyService (no paid crates where they're not allowed: X-ray crates; trading follows
  IsPaidItemTradingAllowed), the Lucky Spin lists every prize in %, the luck passes say what they do in numbers.
