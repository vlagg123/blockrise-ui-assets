## 1. Tutorial (TUTORIAL_V9 → TUTORIAL_V10)
- **Length:** 8 steps (`Config.TutorialSteps = 8`, `RoadTutorialSteps = 8`, `RoadV = 6`).
- **No Company in the tutorial.** At Level 5 the green button "🏢 CREATE YOUR OWN COMPANY" opens the Company window,
  once (`CompanyCtaSeen`).
- **Steps 1–5:** as before (fence, worker, gloves, 10 reps, machine).
- **Step 6, "Build the Garden Shed"** (`Built_shed`):
  - The green button is "▶ BUILD THE GARDEN SHED".
  - It takes the job for you and takes you to the site: `AcceptContract(id, nil, "trip")` → Main `tripTo` → Client
    `firstTrip`.
  - It replaces "your second job", which let players build the fence twice.
- **Step 7, "Train for your next building"** (`Strength` ≥ the Brick Garage's 750):
  - The green button and the hint show the progress, e.g. "💪 TRAIN: 300 / 750 STRENGTH".
  - It ticks on the rep that reaches 750 (Main `roadCount`: a training rep checks a Strength step too). Before the fix
    it waited for the next sync, up to ~4 s.
- **Step 8, "Build the Brick Garage"** (`Taken_garage`):
  - The green button takes the job and teleports you there.
  - The moment it is taken, the tutorial is over: no banner, no green button, no hints, no arrow.
- **After the tutorial nothing says "what to do"** (no yard Strength line, no crate / worker hints). The only exception is
  the Level 5 company button.
- **Migration** (Main `V5.roadMigrate`, `Config.TutorialOldSteps = 7`):
  - inside the old 7-step tutorial you stay on your step;
  - past it, the whole new tutorial counts as done;
  - no reward is paid twice.
- **Teleport (TELEPORT_V9):** your own client makes the trip, so there is no rubber-banding. The server moves you only if
  you are still where you were 3 s later.
- **Daily missions:** no "Mission complete" toast during the tutorial.
