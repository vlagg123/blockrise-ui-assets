## Conclusions
1. Half of the players never start: the first task is an errand (walk to a shop, buy a crate), not the core action;
   4 of them left within 10 s (title screen / loading / "Retrying" are suspects).
2. The tutorial is 7 errands across the map; contracts pay tiny amounts next to the $3,100 start money.
3. The ones who finish the tutorial leave at the first unguided moment (Garden Shed, $190): no big reward, no next goal.
4. Phone/tablet players never got past level 3 (sessions 3-4 min vs 14 min on PC): the 10 mobile UI bugs.
5. The players who pass this point stay a long time and come back (shadowday92): the mid/late loop works.

## Proposed order of work (owner to confirm)
1. Bugs from HANDOFF_2026-10-11 (10 mobile UI fixes, playtime gift, "Retrying").
2. First minute: spawn at the first site, core action in the first 5 s, first payout that matters; crate given after
   the first build and opened with the animation.
3. Tutorial 7 errands → 3 guided steps; gloves / tire flip / excavator / home become optional goals with rewards.
4. End of tutorial: a guaranteed big moment (free crate opening, new-player Cash Rush), always-visible next goal.
5. Server events every 20-30 min with HUD timers (Cash Rush, Luck x2, Gem Rain, Build Frenzy).
6. Funnel steps re-mapped to the Road tutorial steps, so the dashboard shows exactly where people stop.
7. Ads: pause until 1-4 are live; resume without the Rare Hammers creative.
