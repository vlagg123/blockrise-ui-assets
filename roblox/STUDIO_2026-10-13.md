# BlockRise Empire: Studio changes, 2026-10-13 (waiting for the owner's publish)

## TELEPORT_FIX: no more teleport onto the foundation, no more fall under the map
The owner reported this live on 2026-10-12. The green button / first job put him on the corner of the plot. The walls came up on top of him,
pushed him through the ground (the Pads are 0.6 thick and the Ground is 4 thick, top at Y 0), and he fell to
FallenPartsDestroyHeight -500 and respawned at the plaza.
- **Construction (`Construction:StandPoint()`)**
  - Takes the frontmost point of everything the building will ever have (all 8 corners of every add / remove spec, in
    the site origin's space; the front of a plot is local -Z, the origin's LookVector, towards the road).
  - Picks a spot 8 / 11 / 14 / 5 studs beyond it, centred or 5 / 10 studs to the side, never past the site radius − 3.
  - Grounds it with a raycast that ignores Constructions and the characters.
  - Needs a 3×5×3 free box at body height (NPCs are ignored).
  - Returns the ground point and the point to look at (the site origin).
- **Main**
  - `acceptContract` sets `StandPoint` / `StandLook` on the job state right after the crane is placed.
  - `autoFirstJob` stands the new builder there, facing the plot. Before, it used 9 studs from the work point.
- **LocationsUI.Go("site")** (the green NEXT button, PLACES and the rookie trip) uses `StandPoint`, falling back to the
  old rule.
- **Client: the rookie trip** waits for `StandPoint` (it waited for `WorkPoint`).
- **Client: a guard loop every 0.2 s**, which does nothing while seated, dead or within 0.8 s of its last move:
  1. If a collidable piece of a building overlaps your body (a 1.6×1.6×1 box at the HumanoidRootPart), you go to that
     job's `StandPoint`. A job without one (Mega Project, your house) puts you on the ground just outside its radius,
     on your side.
  2. Below Y −8 (under the ground's underside), you are put back at your site's `StandPoint`, else on the ground above
     you (a raycast from Y 300 that ignores Constructions), else at `Map.Spawn`.
- **Tested in Play:**
  - **Every site:** all 18 sites × the 5 buildings of their zone, built instantly. The stand point is outside every
    collidable part (gap at least 3.3 studs, Villa), within the radius and on the ground. 0 bad.
  - **New player:** the first job put him at (−278.3, −70), 21.7 studs in front of Site1, facing it.
  - **GO to the site** from the plaza: the stand point, facing the plot.
  - **Accepting the Garden Shed** from the plaza: the rookie trip went to Site2's stand point.
  - **Under the map** (Y −30 under Site1): back at the stand point after ≤ 1.2 s, health 100.
  - **A 12×6×12 block around the body:** moved to the stand point.
  - **Slabs at the feet / knees and a 6×14×6 block:** physics already pushes the character up or aside, which is fine.
  - **Console:** clean (only the Studio DataStore notices and two errors from my own test calls).
